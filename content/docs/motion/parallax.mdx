# Parallax

## Introduction

**Parallax** moves layers at different rates along shared progress (scroll, drag, or playhead). Near layers travel farther; far layers lag — a depth cue without true 3D.

## Why it matters

- Cheap depth for heroes and storytelling scrolls.
- Easy to overdo — vestibular discomfort and noisy layouts.

## Parameters

| Control | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Progress | Shared 0–1 driver |
| Far / Mid / Near amp | Pixels of travel at progress = 1 |

## Implementation

```js
far.x = progress * farAmp;
near.x = progress * nearAmp;
```

## Key takeaways

- One progress value; many amplitudes.
- Honor reduced motion — disable auto or large amplitudes.
