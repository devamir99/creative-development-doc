# Anticipation & Follow-Through

## Introduction

**Anticipation** moves opposite the main action first (the wind-up). **Follow-through** continues past the target and settles. Together they sell weight and intent.

## Why it matters

- UI that only eases in from zero feels mechanical.
- Overdone wind-up becomes parody — tune to product tone.

## Implementation

Chain three timeline steps: anticipate → action → settle.

## Key takeaways

- Opposite-then-go beats ease-only for many “physical” UI moves.
- Zero anticipation is a valid preset for sober interfaces.
