# Narrative Pacing

## Introduction

**Pacing** is how long each narrative beat is allowed to live before the next cut or reveal. Same structure (setup → turn → payoff) can feel epic or nervous depending on holds.

## Why it matters

- Scroll stories and product launches fail when every beat is the same length.
- Pacing is editing in time — cousin to cut timing.
