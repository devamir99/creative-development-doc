# Critique Loop

## Introduction

A **critique loop** turns opinions into questions against intent. Intent → observation → effect → suggestion → decision.

## Why it matters

- Teams stall on “I don’t like it.”
- Good critiques reference hierarchy, pacing, continuity, and performance — not vibes alone.

## Practical loop

1. **Intent** — What should the viewer understand or feel in 5 seconds?
2. **Observation** — What is actually happening (focal point, cut, ease)?
3. **Effect** — Does that support or fight the intent?
4. **Suggestion** — One concrete change (move subject to thirds; shorten dissolve).
5. **Decision** — Ship, revise, or spike a demo.

## Diagnostic questions

- Where does the eye stop?
- What is the first motion verb?
- Is the cut earning its discontinuity?
- What is the cost (bundle, GPU, vestibular)?
