## Introduction

A **critique loop** turns opinions into questions against intent: Intent → Observation → Effect → Suggestion → Decision.

## Why it matters

- Teams stall on “I don’t like it.”
- Good critiques reference hierarchy, pacing, and continuity.

## Loop

1. **Intent** — What should land in 5 seconds?
2. **Observation** — What is actually happening?
3. **Effect** — Support or fight the intent?
4. **Suggestion** — One concrete change
5. **Decision** — Ship / revise / spike
