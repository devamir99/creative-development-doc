# Leading Lines

## See it first

<LeadingLinesPlayground />

Lines do not need to be drawn. Any edge that converges toward the subject becomes a visual road.

## Introduction

**Leading lines** are real or implied lines that guide the viewer’s eye through the frame, usually toward the focal point.

## Sources of lines

- Architecture: corridors, tables, windows, roads
- Nature: rivers, horizons, shadows, branches
- Bodies: arms, gaze direction, movement vectors
- UI: grids, dividers, progress rails, image perspective
- Typography: baselines and column edges

## Why it matters

Lines give the viewer a route. Without a route, hierarchy relies only on size/contrast. With lines, even a small subject can become inevitable.

## Visual explanation

Converging lines create depth and point to a vanishing/focal area. Curved lines slow the journey; diagonal lines add energy; horizontal lines calm; vertical lines formalize.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Effect |
|---|---|
| Line direction | Destination and emotional energy |
| Convergence | Strength of guidance |
| Interruption | Can create tension or stop the eye |
| Contrast | Stronger lines pull harder |
| Endpoint | Should reward the journey with subject/meaning |

## Practical applications

1. **Product hero:** table edge or shadow points to product.
2. **Case study:** timeline rail leads to outcome metric.
3. **Architecture:** corridor carries viewer to window/person.
4. **Dashboard:** alignment lines guide scanning, though subtlety matters.

## Implementation

In CSS/3D, align perspective, shadows, and grid tracks intentionally. Test by blurring the image: if the route still reads, the lines work.

## Common mistakes

- Lines leading out of the frame
- Multiple strong lines pointing to unrelated subjects
- Lines ending at decorative elements instead of the focal point
- Overusing diagonal stock-photo energy in calm products

## When to use / avoid

Use when depth, journey, or discovery matters. Avoid forcing lines in dense UI where they become visual noise.

## Key takeaways

- Lines are eye routes.
- Their endpoint must matter.
- Convergence creates depth and inevitability.
