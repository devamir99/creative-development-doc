# Orbit Camera

## Introduction

An **orbit** keeps the camera looking at a subject while the eye travels on a sphere (or sphere-like path) around it. Azimuth spins around the vertical axis; elevation climbs or drops; radius is the distance to the target.

FOV can stay fixed so you isolate **spatial** framing — unlike zoom.

## Why it matters

- Product configurators and turntables are orbits (sometimes with easing).
- Studying form: orbit reveals silhouette changes that a locked camera hides.
- In film language, arcs / orbits add energy without cutting — overuse still frets the viewer.

## Visual explanation

```text
            elevation
                ↑
                ● eye (radius)
               ╱
    target ●—————— azimuth around Y
```

Watch the yellow subject and pillars as azimuth changes: parallax proves the eye is moving through space.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Azimuth | Degrees around Y (0 ≈ from +Z) |
| Elevation | Degrees above horizontal through the target |
| Radius | Distance from target to eye |
| FOV | Fixed here (`40°`) so orbit stays spatial |

## Practical examples

1. **Sneaker / watch hero** — slow azimuth ease-in-out loop; modest elevation.
2. **Architecture massing** — wider radius, higher elevation for readable volumes.
3. **Debug a mesh** — orbit beats scrubbing FOV when you need to understand form.

## Implementation

```js
const phi = ((90 - elevationDeg) * Math.PI) / 180;
const theta = (azimuthDeg * Math.PI) / 180;
camera.position.set(
  target.x + radius * Math.sin(phi) * Math.sin(theta),
  target.y + radius * Math.cos(phi),
  target.z + radius * Math.sin(phi) * Math.cos(theta),
);
camera.lookAt(target);
```

`OrbitControls` in drei is fine for apps; this lab exposes the sphere parameters directly for teaching.

## Performance notes

- `frameloop="demand"` while scrubbing; `"always"` only during auto-orbit.
- Cap DPR; one Canvas per demo.

## Common mistakes

- Orbiting while also zooming FOV every frame — hard to read which variable taught you what
- Radius so short the camera clips the subject
- Auto-orbit ignoring `prefers-reduced-motion`

## When to use / avoid

**Use** for form study and product presence.
**Avoid** as endless decoration on content-heavy pages, or when a single authored angle already tells the story.

## Key takeaways

- Orbit = spherical eye move + fixed look-at.
- Keep FOV steady when learning the spatial effect.
- Azimuth / elevation / radius are the three knobs that matter first.
