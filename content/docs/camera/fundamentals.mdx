# Camera Fundamentals

## See it first

Change **shot size** (distance) and **angle** (height). FOV stays locked — so you feel spatial framing, not zoom. You do not need the text below to start learning.

<FundamentalsPlayground />

## Introduction

In film, the **camera** is the audience’s eye. In creative web / real-time 3D, it is the same idea: a view frustum that projects the world onto the screen.

## Why it matters

- Shot size and angle decide power and intimacy before any cut.
- Product heroes fail when distance and height are accidental.
- Shared vocabulary (“wide,” “low angle”) speeds collaboration.
